Aivoid is an AI-assisted learning library. Many guides are generated, reorganized, edited, or refreshed with automated tools.

## How content is created

The site uses content agents to draft technical guides, tutorials, notes, and examples. Those agents may use search results, official documentation, prompts, formatting checks, and post-processing scripts.

## Playful bylines

Some pages use playful bylines such as Banana Rocks, Pixel Mango, Syntax Toast, or Cache Potato.

These are internal content labels, not claims that a real person with that name wrote or manually reviewed the page.

## Review status

Pages may include metadata such as:

- `ai-assisted`: drafted or improved with AI tools.
- `automated-reviewed`: passed automated checks for formatting or basic quality.
- `legacy-import`: older content imported from a previous publishing workflow.

Automated review does not mean human expert verification.

## Freshness

Technology changes quickly. Older pages may be updated, noindexed, archived, or replaced when they become stale.

## Corrections

If you find an error, outdated command, broken link, or unsafe advice, please report it through the contact page.
