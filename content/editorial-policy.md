Aivoid publishes practical technical learning content for developers, students, and builders.

## Publishing goals

Pages should help readers understand a concept, build something, debug a problem, or compare tradeoffs.

We prefer useful explanations, working examples, production notes, and clear limitations over generic summaries.

## AI-assisted workflow

AI tools may help with research, outlines, drafts, examples, formatting, and metadata. Pages are not described as manually reviewed unless that actually happened.

## Legacy content

Older content may remain available for readers while being hidden from search engines or site search. This lets the library keep useful material without promoting stale or repetitive pages.

## Updates and archiving

Pages may be updated, noindexed, archived, merged, or replaced when they are outdated, too thin, duplicated, or no longer useful.

## Advertising

Advertising, if enabled, supports the free site. It does not decide what the site publishes.
